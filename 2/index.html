FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need an appointment?
No — walk-ins are always welcome. For hair straightening or keratin treatments, a quick call ahead is recommended to ensure a stylist is available.
How long does a haircut take?
A standard haircut takes about 20–25 minutes. Haircut + beard together is around 40–50 minutes.
Where exactly are you located?
We're in Oud Metha, Dubai — near Lamcy Plaza and Oud Metha Metro Station. Use the map above for exact directions.
What are your opening hours?
Open daily from 10:00 AM to 1:00 AM — 7 days a week.
Do you offer keratin or hair straightening?
Yes — we offer men's perm and keratin treatments. Call or WhatsApp us to check availability before visiting.

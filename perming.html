Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Will it damage my hair?
No. We use premium, low-alkaline perming products specifically formulated for men's hair that minimize damage while delivering excellent results. Our experienced stylists are trained in proper application techniques that protect your hair's health. We also provide a complete aftercare kit and guidance to keep your hair strong and healthy. Many clients report their hair actually feels thicker and healthier after treatment because the products we use include conditioning agents.
How long does it last?
Your perm will last 3 to 4 months depending on your hair growth rate and how you maintain it. The texture gradually relaxes over time, creating a natural transition rather than an abrupt change. Most clients return every 3-4 months to refresh their look. With proper care using the sulfate-free products we recommend, you can extend the life of your perm even longer.
How much styling effort is needed afterwards?
Very little. That's the beauty of a perm. Your hair will naturally have the volume and texture you want, so styling becomes much easier. Most clients spend just 2-3 minutes in the morning applying a small amount of styling cream and letting their hair air-dry or using a quick blow-dry. We'll teach you the simple styling technique during your appointment. Many clients tell us their morning routine is now 10x faster than before.
Do I need a consultation first?
Not necessarily, but we always start every perm appointment with a complimentary consultation. During this 10-15 minute discussion, we assess your hair type, discuss your desired look, and explain exactly what we'll do. If you want to consult before booking, just message us on WhatsApp with a photo of your hair and we'll let you know if you're a good candidate. Most hair types work great with perms — we'll be honest if yours doesn't.
Is my hair suitable for a perm?
Most hair types are suitable. Perms work best on healthy hair that's at least 4-5 cm long. If your hair is currently bleached, heavily colored, or chemically treated, we may recommend waiting or doing a strand test first. Very fine hair, thick hair, straight hair, wavy hair — we've successfully permed them all. The best way to know for sure is to send us a quick photo on WhatsApp and we'll assess your hair within minutes and give you honest advice.
How long does the session take?
Plan for 90 to 120 minutes (1.5 to 2 hours) at the salon. This includes the initial consultation where we discuss your desired look, the application process, processing time while you relax, neutralizing, washing, and styling. We never rush — we'd rather take the time to do it right. You can bring headphones, use our WiFi, or just relax.
